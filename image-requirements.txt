WEBSITE PHOTO REQUIREMENTS - SHUBHAM PHOTOGRAPHY
As of: 2026-09-18

To build out the photography sections of the website, please send over the
original, high-resolution files below. More resolution than the minimum is
always fine - we crop and optimize everything to fit the site.

WHAT TO SEND
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section                         | How many   | Orientation      | Min size
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Homepage hero banner             | 3          | Landscape, wide  | 2400x1600px
About page portrait              | 1          | Portrait (3:4)   | 1200x1600px
Featured work / portfolio grid   | 6+         | Mostly portrait  | 2000px (short side)
Portfolio galleries (per shoot)  | 6-10 each  | Any              | 2000px (short side)
Client testimonial photos        | 3          | Portrait (3:4-4:5)| 1200x1500px
Instagram feed grid              | 6          | Square (1:1)     | 1080x1080px
Blog cover images                | 1 per post | Landscape (16:9) | 1920x1080px
Video thumbnails / films         | ~5         | Landscape (16:9) | 1920x1080px
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Notes:
- Homepage hero banner: full-bleed background behind bold text - avoid key
  details in the center where the headline sits.
- Featured work / portfolio grid: cropped into a grid layout - extra
  resolution gives room to crop.
- Portfolio galleries: one full set per real wedding/shoot to feature on its
  own page.
- Blog cover images: also shown cropped to portrait on the blog list page.
- Video thumbnails / films: real video files if the films are ready,
  otherwise frame grabs are fine.

FILE GUIDELINES
- Format: JPEG or high-quality WEBP (avoid PNG for photos - larger files, no
  real benefit)
- Color profile: sRGB
- Send full, uncropped originals where possible - the site crops responsively
  per layout
- No watermarks or text overlays
- No hard file-size limit, but keep individual files under ~20MB

HOW TO SEND THEM
A shared Google Drive or WeTransfer folder works best, organized into
subfolders matching the sections above (e.g. "Hero", "Portfolio - Aura &
James", "Instagram", "Blog").
